# Harbor Goods FAQ

Welcome to Harbor Goods. Below are answers to common customer questions about orders, shipping, and support.

## Returns

We accept returns within **30 days** of delivery for most items in unused condition with original packaging. Start a return from your order history or contact support with your order number.

## Shipping

Standard shipping typically arrives in **3–5 business days** within the continental United States. Expedited options may be available at checkout. Processing time is usually one business day before your package leaves our warehouse.

## Order tracking

After your order ships, you will receive an email with a **tracking link**. You can also view tracking details anytime by signing in to your account and opening the order.

## Customer support hours

Our team is available **Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Eastern Time**. Email and chat are monitored during these hours; we respond to messages received outside business hours on the next business day.
